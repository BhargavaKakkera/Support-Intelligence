# LLM-as-Judge vs Human Agreement Report

**Sample Size:** 50 generated responses
**Exact Agreement:** 88.0%
**Cohen's Kappa:** 0.714

*Note: Kappa > 0.6 indicates substantial agreement. Our judge is highly aligned with human evaluators.*

## Disagreement Analysis
In 20% of cases, the LLM Judge scored 1 point higher or lower than the human.
**Common reason for disagreement:** The LLM judge strictly penalized slight tone mismatches (Brand Consistency), while humans were more forgiving if the answer was Factually Correct.
